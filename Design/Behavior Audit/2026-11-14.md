# Calendar and task-bank behavior audit

Date: November 14, 2026.

## Implemented consistency fixes

- Plain-clicking a selected bank task preserves the rest of its selection; Command-click toggles membership. Selecting a bank task also updates the default creation calendar.
- Dragging a selection preserves all selected tasks. Bank reorder moves the group in bank order. Dropping on the calendar previews and schedules the tasks consecutively, retaining each duration and calendar.
- Group reorder and group scheduling create one undo action. If scheduling fails partway, successfully scheduled tasks remain undoable together; the others remain in the bank and the error reports the partial result.
- Expanded cards and collapsed thumbnails share Edit, Copy, Cut, Paste, and Delete actions. A context action on an unselected task selects that task; actions on an existing selection preserve the group.
- Clicking empty calendar space clears bank focus and selection so clipboard/delete shortcuts target the calendar. Clicking blank bank creation space clears task selection.
- Copy/paste works bank-to-bank, bank-to-calendar, and calendar-to-bank. Copies retain notes, calendar, and duration; pasted calendar events are individual events without copied invitation/recurrence behavior.
- Cut checks clipboard success before deleting bank tasks.
- Bank titles wrap. Notes use a growing multiline editor, with Enter inserting a newline. Bank note previews strip HTML and use faded calendar-colored text.
- Selected bank cards use calendar-colored outlines and stronger fills. Their accent runs the full height. Insertions/deletions use a short transition respecting Reduce Motion.
- Bank resizing continues to use calendar height scale, 10-minute snapping, Shift for 1-minute precision, optimistic revision checking, and undo.

## Intentional differences

- Bank tasks are unscheduled: no start/end date, all-day status, recurrence, attendees, invitation response, or read-only calendar permissions until scheduled.
- A bank group has an order rather than original event start times, so scheduling a group lays its tasks out consecutively.
- Calendar and bank editing both use anchored popovers, click-away saving, explicit Save/Cancel, and Escape cancellation. The bank uses estimated duration instead of scheduled date/time fields.
- Clicking a collapsed thumbnail opens the bank, preserving the requested ribbon behavior; dragging it still schedules the bank selection.

## Verification

The signed app builds successfully. All 397 automated behavior checks pass, including group reorder/undo, cross-area clipboard preservation, cut/undo, resize arithmetic, and existing calendar/history behavior. Checks use sample events and temporary bank storage, not the user's calendars.

Pointer gesture routing, live EventKit group scheduling, and animation appearance have been code-reviewed but have not been verified through a live UI interaction in this audit.
